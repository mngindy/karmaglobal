Quality & Compliance

Certified to meet global agricultural safety and international trade regulations.

At Karma Global, product safety and quality are at the core of our operations. We adhere strictly to international agricultural and export standards to guarantee that our fresh produce reaches destination ports in optimal condition.

GLOBALG.A.P.

GLOBALG.A.P. Certified

Ensures Good Agricultural Practices across our partner farms, focusing on food safety, trace-ability, environmental protection, and worker welfare.

ISO 22000

Food Safety Management

Certifies that our entire handling and packing chain adheres to comprehensive food safety management system requirements.

BRCGS

Global Standard for Food Safety

Demonstrates operational compliance in hygiene, packaging integrity, and quality assurance for international retail distribution.

PHYTO

Phytosanitary Certification

Issued by official quarantine authorities to verify that every export shipment is free from regulated pests and plant diseases.

Export Documentation Included With Every Shipment

  • Certificate of Origin: Authenticated proof of harvest region.
  • Bill of Lading (B/L): Full shipping line tracking documentation.
  • Phytosanitary Certificate: Official plant health clearance.
  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List: Detailed container breakdowns.
